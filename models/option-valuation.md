\# Option Valuation



Every proposal must include:



1\. Current underlying price and timestamp

2\. Bid, ask and expected limit price

3\. Strike, expiration and days remaining

4\. Breakeven and collateral requirement

5\. Profit and loss under base and stress scenarios

6\. Independent expected-return assumptions

7\. Expected value after spread, fees and cash yield

8\. Kelly result and quarter-Kelly position

9\. Assignment exposure

10\. Exit conditions



Contracts with the same expiration must use one consistent forecast distribution.

Market IV must not automatically be treated as the physical forecast.
